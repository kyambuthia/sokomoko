# Roadmap

The maintained [production and feature roadmap](roadmap/ROADMAP.md) records
completed PostgreSQL and deployment work and remaining product work.

The versioned JSON API is integrated with PostgreSQL. Partner account membership
and catalog/order authorization are supported; full vendor tenancy still needs
scoped settings, mixed-order fulfillment, onboarding, and settlement.

Priorities are real online payments when required, business shipping/tax/legal
policies, durable notifications, and validation in the target deployment.
